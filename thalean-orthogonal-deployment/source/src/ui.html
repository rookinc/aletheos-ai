Orthogonal assembly

Three copies of the Data view from Thalean Graph Explorer v0.34.0, centred on one origin and rotated into the YZ, ZX and XY planes.

Each copy retains the same 9,000 native states, 45,000 edges and 450 twenty-step F cycles. The on-screen paths use the original sampling of the dense graph.

Geometry and dynamics. The rotations preserve distances and handedness. Merge changes the original split chart, from separated sheets at 0% to a joined torus at 100%. The coordinates are a display embedding; placing three charts in physical coordinate planes does not establish a physical interaction law. Copies remain uncoupled.

The original PID comparison is retained: P 1.4, I 0.12, D 4. Its response uses model time; the Hz control sets display cycles per second.

Settings. Save/Load retains camera, plane visibility, phase offsets and display settings. Loading starts a fresh simulation.
