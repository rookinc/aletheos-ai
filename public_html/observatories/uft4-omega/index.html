About this instrument

The certified native G60 graph has 60 vertices, 120 edges, and degree four. Its 15 disjoint four-vertex fibers project to G15, with 15 vertices and 30 edges.

The G900 host contains 15 native G60 blocks with 1,800 local and 1,800 external edges. The mirrored view starts with each G60 at an outer end and its own G15 sheet at the central interface. Merge halves brings the display positions together while retaining each block and quotient identity. The value is retained but inactive in overview or with the host hidden. The two quotients remain distinct even where their drawings coincide: this is a display reflection, not a topological merge or a quotient identification induced by the external pairing. The native 60-edge matching between adjacent blocks is unchanged. One torus is split at its local midplane across A/source and B/neighbor, with each half sampled from the same field. This display partition introduces neither a second field source nor a new coupling. Pulse arrival measures unit graph hops using a bounded diagnostic clock. Seeking and repeating the pulse do not change UFT4 time.

Keep three objects distinct: the native graph, its prescribed toroidal embedding, and Joachim’s proposed continuous UFT4 field. Rotation is a display choice; the field visualization is not a validated physical model.

Sunlight view increases visual contrast independently of Sheets, Plasma or Hybrid. Switching back restores the selected appearance settings.
