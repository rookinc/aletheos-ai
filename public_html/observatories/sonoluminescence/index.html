Model and interpretation

Radial motion: a spherical bubble driven by a sinusoidal acoustic pressure. Surface tension, viscosity, and the declared gas closure enter the radial equation.

Thermodynamics: any displayed temperature is inferred from the selected closure. It is not a measured interior temperature.

Boundary-flux comparison: the scalar-collapse and gate-weighted scores illustrate how collapse strength and admissibility can differ. The chosen product of recurrence, shape, and material factors has not been fitted to observations.

The luminous marker highlights collapse schematically. This model does not determine an emission spectrum, photon yield, or microscopic light-producing mechanism.
