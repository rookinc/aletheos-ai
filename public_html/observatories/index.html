Certification and Mathematical Integrity

Where available, reproducible certificates document verified invariants, source fingerprints, computational audits, and the precise scope of established results. Certification distinguishes demonstrated finite mathematics from experimental hypotheses and open physical interpretations. A certificate applies to its specified construction and verification scope, not automatically to every feature of an observatory.

For example, G9000 Transport Certificate 001 records 20 passing checks, including exact native transport identity, twenty-step return, and registered-history replay. Upstream history-producer provenance remains open.
