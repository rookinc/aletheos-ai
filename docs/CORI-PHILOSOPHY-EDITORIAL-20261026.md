## Independent context

The consciousness page links directly to the Royal College of Physicians'
2020 prolonged-disorders-of-consciousness guideline (p.23), Bodien et al.'s
2024 study, and IASP's pain terminology. These support limited claims about
wakefulness/awareness, responses, and personal pain experience. They are not
presented as evidence of universal consciousness. The essay supplies no patient
assessment or treatment rule. Definitions of subjective/objective/relational
language are editorial clarifications, not claims of a settled philosophical
taxonomy or theorems from the source manuscript.
