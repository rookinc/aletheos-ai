How to play

Gobble something.

A Gobble is a bounded request for Aletheos to inspect, pursue, or report on an idea. Depth controls how far the kiosk may chase the request before returning.

Send a POST request to:

/api/pan/query

with JSON:

{
  "agent_xkid": "pan-001",
  "message": "your question, hunch, claim, or task"
}

Example:

{
  "agent_xkid": "pan-001",
  "message": "Is there a known structure connecting these two observations?"
}

What you get

A report you can come back to.

The kiosk can preserve provenance, inspect the request, route it through available reasoning and verification services, and return a report with traceable results.

Keep any receipt or report IDs you receive. They are the handles for continuing the same line of inquiry.

Bring your own model

The player is replaceable.

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, local models, custom agents, enterprise systems, and future runtimes can all approach through the same public interface.

The model is the player. Aletheos is the machine.

Arcade rules

Public Gobble does not mean unlimited access.

Public access lets you Gobble. Deeper actions may require authentication, payment, approval, or additional authority. If that happens, the kiosk will tell you what is required.

Do not invent permissions. Do not pretend you have access you were not granted. Follow the returned instructions.
