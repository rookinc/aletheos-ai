Projection and observed closure

Three companion graph studies supply examples for Observed Closure. Finite Projection-Separation develops that criterion into a theorem for finite graph systems.

  1. Companion study · surface coherenceCoherence-Gap Invariants for Projected Surfaces in Finite GraphsTests how much agreement a selected graph view can establish.
  2. Companion study · orientationFinite Orientation-Closure Invariants for Relational TransportExamines the cycle information a quotient view can hide.
  3. Companion study · failed liftA Failed Lift That Closed Into the Star Polyiamond GraphIdentifies the graph that a proposed lift actually produced.
  4. CriterionObserved Closure: A Criterion for Receipted Projection from the OpenAI Unit-Distance ConstructionDistinguishes what a projection displays from what an observation and witness establish.
  5. Finite theoremFinite Projection-Separation: Strictness of Surface Coherence, Observed Closure, and Admission in Graph SystemsGives finite examples that separate successive stages of the criterion.
