# CoRI public remit and Rook boundary

Date: 26 October 2026. Status: source revision prepared for review; not deployed
to aletheos.ai. The existing site layout is retained for this editorial pass.

## Governing direction

CoRI's value to its primary audience, the concerned public, is in its findings
and their significance. Its remit includes AI stewardship, philosophy, and
scientific research across disciplines. Thalean research is one program.
Methods and tools serve the findings and remain available to interested readers
and researchers who want to employ them.

Use the organizational attribution **Center of Recursive Inquiry — a Rook
Services Inc. initiative**.

| CoRI / Aletheos | Rook / rook.ca |
| --- | --- |
| Findings, philosophical arguments, public explanations, open questions | Commercial engineering, product capabilities, implementations, evaluations |
| AI stewardship: agency, responsibility, social consequences, governance inquiry | Specific information controls, policy enforcement and disclosure mechanisms |
| Science and philosophy as independent fields of inquiry | Wombat, StaticHouse, integrations and product readiness |
| Papers and methods as supporting resources | Evidence for particular product claims and operating boundaries |

Shared questions do not require duplicate missions. CoRI can study governance
and accountability while Rook develops mechanisms for particular applications.

## Live-site review

The live rook.ca homepage was inspected in Chrome, along with its About route.
Its focus is AI conformance, information qualification, controlled disclosure,
and bounded evaluation. That already fits Rook's commercial role. The About
route describes CoRI only through mathematics of information and relation;
that short reference should be widened to the three-part remit above in a
future Rook source update. No rook.ca files were changed in this revision.

The previous Aletheos homepage/footer described CoRI as an AI think tank.
The homepage, research index, About and Mission foregrounded methodology and
directed visitors through Thalean vocabulary. These institutional descriptions
and the corresponding glossary/graph-page scope text have been revised.

## Public reading experience

The homepage now opens with an actual bounded result, then introduces the
institution and its wider questions. Primary navigation leads to findings,
About and Mission. Labs, evidence and technical resources remain reachable in
the footer and research index. Existing technical routes and native simulator
code are preserved.

The new memory article uses a repeatable form: what happened; why it matters;
what remains uncertain; optional methods and source records. Its counts come
from `native-terrain/deployment/served-browser-receipt-20261026.json` at commit
`0509cc6b1b62a75bbf7e149be2235ae87ead659f`. It describes an intentionally
implemented rule and one tested configuration, without claiming novel physics
or a result about human memory or a particular AI product.

The next editorial priority is substantive public reading in AI stewardship
and philosophy, grounded in actual work. Research remit and open questions
must not be presented as completed findings. A verified route for public
questions also remains to be identified; none was invented.
