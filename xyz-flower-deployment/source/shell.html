The Flower of Life view
Nineteen equal circles are placed on a triangular lattice: one central circle, six around it, and twelve in the outer ring. Each native route is assigned a circle by route index modulo 19. The XYZ flower
The 3D layout places a full 19-circle flower in each of the XY, XZ and YZ planes, meeting at one origin. Each native route belongs to one plane by route index modulo three: 150 routes per plane, 450 total. No darts are duplicated. Plane colors identify the assigned plane. Bloom depth offsets each frame along that plane’s normal.
Plane visibility affects drawing only; hidden routes continue to advance. The three perpendicular flowers form a display arrangement, not a newly verified native adjacency or a Fourier correspondence. Intersections do not switch routes.
