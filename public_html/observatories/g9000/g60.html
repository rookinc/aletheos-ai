The carrier uses the atlas’s actual G60 adjacency: 60 vertices, 120 undirected edges, four neighbors per vertex. Changing the view moves the drawing, not its connections.

The forward display runs clockwise from the top; the return retraces it. +4 and −4 retain the ab / ba word distinction. This does not assert ba = −ab.

What is illustrative?

Pole placement, field curves, geometric layouts and the moving marker are display conventions. The marker follows the signed register, not a verified walk along G60 edges. The E / E⁻¹ envelope is shown symbolically; no numerical E operator or magnetic dynamics are claimed.

Red marks the two north regions; blue marks the two south regions. The + / − labels instead mark forward and return presentation.
