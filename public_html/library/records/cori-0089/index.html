Using the instrument

The paired-port experiment compares two routes that reach the same projected endpoint on opposite lifts. Compare signed and ordinary readings, then turn to oriented energy current, a CMB receiver, an exterior cut modeled for Sagittarius A*, or two synthetic cardiac drives. The field guide gives the preparation, interventions, units, and scope of each replay.
