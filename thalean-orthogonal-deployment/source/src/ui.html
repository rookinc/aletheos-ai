Geometry and dynamics. The rotations preserve distances and handedness. Merge changes the original split chart, from separated sheets at 0% to a joined torus at 100%. The coordinates are a display embedding; placing three charts in physical coordinate planes does not establish a physical interaction law. Copies remain uncoupled.

Inverse cube. A 50 × 50 × 50 grid samples the central cube [−0.5, 0.5]³. A cell is shown only when its centre is within the selected tolerance of each of the three complete native F-path polylines. The volume is a finite-resolution estimate in chart units cubed. The wire cube marks the observation window. It is not a claim that a bounded physical cube has emerged. Hiding any copy removes the three-way overlap. Factor links and PID amplitude are not part of the overlap test.

Physical interpretation. A Planck-scale cell and a Hilbert action are proposed interpretations. Establishing them would require a physical scale and a defined action. This view supplies spatial evidence for examining that proposal.
