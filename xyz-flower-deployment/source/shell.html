MODEL NOTES

One transport.
Several ways to see it.

The dart motion is the registered F permutation from Thalean Graph Explorer v0.34.0: 9,000 states, partitioned into 450 routes of 20 steps. All routes are reconstructed and checked at startup.

The mandala is a drawing choice

Five concentric palace levels evoke body, speech, mind, wisdom and bliss. We place the five transport frames on these five levels and the four carrier phases around each level. This is an experimental embedding, not a traditional deity-to-state correspondence. Direction markers follow the source's role positions; this drawing uses E at the right.

At each seat, the text describes an embracing couple. The eight-role graph identifies repeated names across seats. Its ring-neighbor edges are inferred; its partner relations are documented. Equal unit weights produce the cube graph.

The Flower of Life view

Nineteen equal circles are placed on a triangular lattice: one central circle, six around it, and twelve in the outer ring. Each native route is assigned a circle by route index modulo 19. Alternating routes travel in opposite directions. A full turn displays the route’s 20 registered F states; arc interpolation supplies the drawing between them.

Bloom depth separates the five transport frames vertically. Circle assignments, rotation directions and frame heights are display choices. Circle intersections do not transfer a dart to a different route, and this view makes no new Fourier or topological equivalence claim.

The XYZ flower

The 3D layout places a full 19-circle flower in each of the XY, XZ and YZ planes, meeting at one origin. Each native route belongs to one plane by route index modulo three: 150 routes per plane, 450 total. No darts are duplicated. Plane colors identify the assigned plane. Bloom depth offsets each frame along that plane’s normal.

Plane visibility affects drawing only; hidden routes continue to advance. The three perpendicular flowers form a display arrangement, not a newly verified native adjacency or a Fourier correspondence. Intersections do not switch routes.

What the Fourier view verifies

The Pilot 002 matrices satisfy LG60P = PLcube and QP = I₈. Select a cube mode to see its actual signed amplitude lifted into G60. The G60 placement is a display layout; its 120 edges are native. The basis-selected transfer is not a local vertex assignment. This Laplacian relation does not assert that F commutes with P.

Requiring graph antipodes too limits the transferable rank to five. Changing to normalized Laplacians leaves only the constant overlap. No physical or religious equivalence is established.

Controls & state

Cycle rate means complete 20-step returns per second. The clock uses elapsed time; drawing interpolation does not alter states. Saved JSON resumes settings, camera and transport phase. No PID or water solver is included. Flower of Life, Native F and Mandala share the same route IDs and motion.
